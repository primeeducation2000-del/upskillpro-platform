# UpSkillPro Instagram Weekly Growth Plan

## Weekly Posting Plan

Post 3 times per week:

1. Monday: Learner English tip
2. Wednesday: Workplace/sector English tip
3. Friday: Employer or soft skills post

## Reel 1: 3 English Phrases For Hotel Reception

What to record:
- Use your phone camera.
- Speak for 20-30 seconds.
- Show each phrase on screen.

Script:
`Here are 3 useful English phrases for hotel reception.`

Phrase 1:
`Good morning, welcome to the hotel. How can I help you?`

Phrase 2:
`May I take your name and booking reference, please?`

Phrase 3:
`Your room is ready. The lift is on your left.`

Caption:
`3 useful English phrases for hotel reception staff. Save this if you work in hospitality or want to improve workplace English.`

Hashtags:
`#HospitalityEnglish #HotelEnglish #EnglishForWork #ESOL #ESPEnglish #UpSkillPro`

## Reel 2: How To Introduce Yourself At Work

Script:
`Here is a simple way to introduce yourself at work.`

Beginner:
`Hello, my name is Ali. I work in housekeeping. Nice to meet you.`

More professional:
`Good morning, my name is Ali. I am part of the housekeeping team. Please let me know if you need any help.`

Caption:
`A simple workplace introduction can sound more professional with the right phrases.`

Hashtags:
`#EnglishForWork #WorkplaceEnglish #LearnEnglish #ESOL #CommunicationSkills #UpSkillPro`
