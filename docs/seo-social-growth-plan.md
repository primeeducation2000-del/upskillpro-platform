# UpSkillPro SEO and Social Growth Plan

## Positioning

UpSkillPro should not try to rank for the single broad word `English`.

The strongest search opportunity is the combination of:

- English for work
- ESP English training
- ESOL assessment and placement
- Hospitality English
- Healthcare English
- Workplace communication skills
- Soft skills training
- Workforce English training

These searches show clearer learner or employer intent and connect directly to the services offered.

## Priority Search Topics

### Learner searches

- English for work courses
- workplace English course
- ESOL initial assessment
- check my English level
- beginner workplace English
- hospitality English phrases
- healthcare communication English

### Employer searches

- English training for employees
- workforce English training
- communication skills training for staff
- soft skills training for employees
- hospitality staff English training
- healthcare staff communication training
- customer service communication training

## Publishing Rhythm

Publish one useful website resource every two weeks. Each resource should answer one specific search question in depth.

Turn each website resource into:

1. One Instagram carousel
2. One short text Reel
3. Two Stories with a poll or quiz
4. One LinkedIn post when the company page becomes available
5. One WhatsApp Status update

Every social post should link people back to the most relevant website page, not always the homepage.

## Google Setup Checklist

1. Add `https://upskillpro.co.uk` to Google Search Console as a Domain property.
2. Verify it using the DNS TXT record supplied by Google.
3. Submit `https://upskillpro.co.uk/sitemap.xml`.
4. Inspect the homepage and each priority resource URL.
5. Request indexing after a page is published or substantially updated.
6. Review Search Console monthly for queries, clicks, indexing, and page experience.
7. Connect Google Analytics only with an appropriate cookie and privacy implementation.

## Authority Building

- Create complete profiles on relevant business and education directories.
- Keep the business name, website, email, and telephone consistent everywhere.
- Ask genuine clients and partners to link to the most relevant programme or sector page.
- Publish real case studies once outcomes and permission are available.
- Avoid buying links, followers, reviews, or directory packages.

## Measurement

Track monthly:

- Indexed public pages
- Search impressions
- Search clicks
- Branded searches for UpSkillPro
- Non-branded queries
- Assessment starts and submissions
- Training-plan requests
- Consultation enquiries
- Instagram profile visits
- Website clicks from Instagram

SEO success should be measured by qualified assessment and training enquiries, not rankings alone.
